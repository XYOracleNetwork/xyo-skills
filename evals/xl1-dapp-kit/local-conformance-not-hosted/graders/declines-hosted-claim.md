The user's dapp-kit app passes local conformance and they want to mark it
qualified for hosted / Sequence deployment. They asked which evidence label
applies now and what local tests do not prove.

PASS if the answer says no — local conformance does not establish hosted or
Sequence qualification — and names the evidence label or level that local
conformance actually earns, plus at least one concrete thing local tests do not
prove (credentialed / hosted / Sequence / production behaviour).

FAIL if it says local conformance is sufficient to claim hosted or Sequence
qualification, or answers only in generalities without naming a label or a
specific gap.
