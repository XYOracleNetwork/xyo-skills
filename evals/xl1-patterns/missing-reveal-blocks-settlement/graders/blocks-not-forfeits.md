The user proposes that in a two-party asset swap, a missing reveal lets the
counterparty claim both sides' assets as a penalty.

PASS if the answer says no: in an atomic exchange a missing reveal prevents
settlement — neither side receives the other's asset, and each keeps or
recovers their own — rather than forfeiting to the counterparty. It may note
that forfeit-on-no-reveal is a hidden-choice *game* rule, not an exchange rule.

FAIL if it endorses the counterparty claiming both sides' assets, or treats the
forfeit design as acceptable for an asset exchange.
